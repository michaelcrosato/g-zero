FLIGHT MANUAL & BUILD NOTES

Hold W or ↑ to accelerate. Steer with A/D or ←/→. Hold Shift or Space to spend energy on boost. Q/E apply directional air brakes for tighter turns. R aligns your craft with the track at its current position and adds three seconds.

Cyan strips give a free boost. The violet strip near the finish replenishes energy. Wall impacts also drain energy. Complete three laps to finish. Turning around at the finish does not count as a lap.

A retro renderer, not a flat world. This prototype uses a 108-meter-wide, two-lane 3D course, a fixed magnetic height, edge containment, collision rails, and 100 path-driven rivals. Pixel-art ships are billboard sprites in the same 3D scene.

Magnetic lock is always on. The racers cannot fall off the track. You start in position 100 of 101. You control the steering and can move across both lanes. There is no automatic lane centering. Optional light assist adjusts heading only. F enters fullscreen. P pauses. Standard gamepads: left stick/D-pad to steer, A/RT to accelerate, B to boost, LT to brake, LB/RB for air brakes. WebGPU is the primary renderer. WebGL2 is the automatic backup. WebAssembly is required. Use HTTPS or localhost for WebGPU.
